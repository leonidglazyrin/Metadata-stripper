Metadata Stripper

Drop photos or videos to remove EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC, ICC, C2PA / Content Credentials, maker notes, container tags, chapters and telemetry tracks.

How it works

  • Images are decoded to raw pixels and written out as a brand-new file, so no metadata segment of any kind (EXIF, XMP, IPTC, ICC, C2PA/JUMBF, comments, maker notes, thumbnails) survives. Orientation is baked into the pixels first. HEIC is output as JPEG.
  • Videos are remuxed with only the audio/video/subtitle streams kept; global, per-stream and chapter metadata, data/telemetry/GPS tracks, cover art and C2PA boxes are dropped.
  • Files are processed on the server in a temp folder and deleted immediately after the response.
  • Invisible pixel-level watermarks (e.g. SynthID) aren't metadata — they're woven into the image/audio content itself — and this tool does not attempt to remove them.
